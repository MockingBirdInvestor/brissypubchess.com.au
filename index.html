About

Brissy Pub Chess is a casual night of blitz chess every Wednesday at Sea Legs Brewing Co, Kangaroo Point.

All levels welcome. Just walk up, register and play.

FAQ

When and where?

Every Wednesday, Sea Legs Brewing Co, 89 Main St, Kangaroo Point. Rego from 6pm, games from 6:30pm.

How much?

$15, or $10 concession. Includes a drink or a snack.

How do I pay?

Tap and go with your card or phone.

Do I need to book?

No, just walk up and register on the night.

Is it for beginners or experienced players?

Both. All levels welcome.

Can kids come?

Under-18s are welcome if accompanied by an adult.

How long are the games?

3+2 blitz: 3 minutes each, plus 2 seconds added after every move.

How long does the tournament take?

Generally an hour or less, usually about five games.

Who will I play?

Each round you're paired with someone on a similar score. When numbers are small, it switches to making sure everyone plays everyone.

How is it scored?

A win is 1 point and a draw is half a point. Players on equal points are split by number of wins, otherwise they share the place. There's also a Pub Score, where beating a stronger player counts for more than beating a weaker one.

Are there prizes?

No, bragging rights only.

Do I need to bring a board?

No. Boards and clocks are supplied.
